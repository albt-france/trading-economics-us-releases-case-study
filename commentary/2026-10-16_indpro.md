# U.S. industrial production rose 0.7% in September

Industrial production rose 0.7% in September from the previous month, according to the Federal Reserve, after a revised 0.1% gain in August, first reported as unchanged. Output was up 2.2% from a year earlier. The monthly gain was well above the model forecast (illustrative) of 0.1%. The breakdown by manufacturing, mining and utilities is in the Fed's release. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield fell 3 bp to 4.74% and the Nasdaq Composite fell 2.15%. WTI crude oil rose 3.34% to 108.21 dollars a barrel.
