# U.S. producer prices rose 1.3% in September

The producer price index (PPI) for final demand rose 1.3% in September from the previous month, after an upwardly revised 0.5% in August. From a year earlier, producer prices rose 6.3%. The monthly rise was far above the model forecast (illustrative) of 0.1%. It came a day after a CPI report in which energy prices rose sharply. Retail sales for September were published at the same time. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield fell 5 bp to 4.77%, and WTI crude oil rose 8.69% to 104.71 dollars a barrel.
