# U.S. payrolls rose 29,000 in September; unemployment rate up to 4.2%

Non-farm payrolls rose 29,000 in September, below the model forecast (illustrative) of 71,000 and the revised 133,000 in August. August was first reported at 162,000. Net revisions totalled -60,000 over July and August. The unemployment rate rose to 4.2% from 4.1%. Average hourly earnings rose 0.1% from the previous month, after 0.3% in August. Hourly earnings were up 3.0% from a year earlier. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield rose 5 bp to 4.83% and the broad dollar index fell 0.33%. The Nasdaq Composite gained 1.19%. The report showed hiring losing pace after the Fed's September rate increase.
