# U.S. consumer prices rose 0.4% in October; core CPI up 0.3%

The consumer price index (CPI) rose 0.4% in October from the previous month, after 0.6% in September. Core CPI rose 0.3%, the same pace as in September. Core CPI leaves out food and energy. Energy prices rose 0.7% in October, a smaller gain than the month before. Food prices rose 0.5%. Shelter rose 0.3%. Year-on-year rates cannot be computed: the October 2025 index was never published (2025 lapse in appropriations). On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield rose 17 bp to 4.73% and the 10-year yield rose 10 bp to 4.98%. The broad dollar index gained 0.37% and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.74%.
