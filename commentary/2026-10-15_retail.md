# U.S. retail sales rose 0.8% in September

Retail sales rose 0.8% in September from the previous month, after an upwardly revised 1.3% in August, first reported at 1.1%. Retail sales excluding autos rose 0.9%, after a revised 1.4% in August. From a year earlier, retail sales were up 6.3%. The figures are in current dollars, so part of the gain reflects higher prices, notably for gasoline. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield fell 5 bp to 4.77% and the broad dollar index fell 0.36%. WTI crude oil rose 8.69% to 104.71 dollars a barrel. Producer prices for September were published at the same time.
