# U.S. economy grew at a 3.4% annualized rate in the third quarter

Real GDP grew at a 3.4% annualized rate in the third quarter of 2026, according to the advance estimate from the Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA), up from 2.2% in the second quarter. The headline is the change in inflation-adjusted output from the previous quarter, expressed at an annual rate. The September income and spending report was published at the same time. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield fell 6 bp to 4.61% and the 10-year yield fell 7 bp to 4.98%. The Nasdaq Composite fell 1.38%. The advance estimate will be revised twice in the coming months.
