# U.S. initial jobless claims rose to 200,000

Initial claims for unemployment benefits rose to 200,000 in the week ended November 7, up from a revised 191,000 the previous week. Claims were in line with their 12-week average of 200,000 and remain low, a sign that layoffs are limited. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield rose 13 bp to 4.86% and the 10-year yield rose 12 bp to 5.10%. The broad dollar index gained 0.35% and EUR/USD fell 0.87%. Claims are the only weekly read on the labor market; the next report is due on November 19.
