# U.S. job openings rose to 7.52 million in September

Job openings rose to 7.52 million at the end of September, from a revised 7.19 million in August, according to the Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (JOLTS). August was first reported at 7.08 million. The survey describes labor demand one month before the payroll report published later the same week. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield rose 2 bp to 4.61% and the 10-year yield rose 4 bp to 4.95%. The broad dollar index gained 0.36% and the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.83%.
