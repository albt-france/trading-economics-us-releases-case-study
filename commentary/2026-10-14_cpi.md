# U.S. consumer prices rose 0.6% in September and 3.7% from a year earlier

The consumer price index (CPI) rose 0.6% in September from the previous month, after 0.4% in August. From a year earlier, prices rose 3.7%, up from 3.4%. Energy prices rose 5.2% on the month and drove the headline. Core CPI rose 0.3% for a second month. From a year earlier, core prices rose 2.6%, up from 2.4%. Core CPI leaves out food and energy. Shelter rose 0.4%. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield rose 3 bp to 4.82%, the broad dollar index gained 0.46% and the Nasdaq Composite fell 1.88%. Headline inflation is now well above the Fed's 2% goal, mainly because of energy.
