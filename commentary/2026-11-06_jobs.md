# U.S. payrolls rose 165,000 in October; unemployment rate fell to 4.1%

Non-farm payrolls rose 165,000 in October, well above the model forecast (illustrative) of 51,000 and the revised 60,000 in September. September was first reported at 29,000. Net revisions totalled +84,000 over August and September. The unemployment rate fell to 4.1% from 4.2%. Average hourly earnings rose 0.4% from the previous month, after a revised 0.3% in September. Hourly earnings were up 3.1% from a year earlier. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield rose 5 bp to 4.51% and the broad dollar index gained 0.31%. With the revisions, the three-month average gain rose to 137,000.
