# Fed held its target range at 3.75%-4.00% in October

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) held the target range for the federal funds rate at 3.75%-4.00% on 28 October, after raising it at its September meeting. The meeting had no new Summary of Economic Projections; the next set comes with the December meeting. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield fell 3 bp to 4.67%, while the 10-year yield rose 6 bp to 5.05%, a steeper curve. The broad dollar index rose 0.15% and the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.27%. WTI crude oil rose 2.79%. The statement, the vote and the press conference are on the Federal Reserve website.
