# U.S. PCE inflation rose to 3.6% in September; core PCE at 3.1%

The personal consumption expenditures (PCE) price index rose 3.6% from a year earlier in September, up from 3.4% in August. Prices rose 0.5% from the previous month. Core PCE, which leaves out food and energy, rose 0.3% from the previous month. From a year earlier, core PCE rose 3.1%, up from 3.0% in August. Core PCE for August, first reported at 0.2%, was revised to 0.3%. Personal spending rose 0.5% from the previous month, after 0.9% in August. On the day, the 2-year Treasury yield fell 6 bp to 4.61%. Core PCE is the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, and it remains above the 2% target.
